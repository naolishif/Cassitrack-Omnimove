    How your Eco Score works
    Every greener trip you take turns into points.
    1The Green Index of a single trip

    For every route we estimate the CO₂ of the option you choose and compare it with the same trip made by car. The greener the option, the higher the index — from 0 to 100.

    CO₂ = emission factor × distance (km)
    Green Index = 100 − (CO₂ ÷ CO₂ by car) × 100
    2CO₂ per transport mode
    Mode CO₂ / km Index
    🚶 Walking0 g100
    🚲 Cycling0 g100
    🛴 E-scooter0 g100
    🚌 Urban bus68 g60
    🚗 Private car170 g0

    Emission factors from the European Environment Agency; the bus value is per passenger-km.

    3Combined journeys

    A combined route mixes modes, so no single factor describes it. Each leg is charged at its own rate — the bus kilometres emit, the ones by bike, e-scooter or on foot do not — and the index is then worked out over the whole distance, clean kilometres included.

    CO₂ = Σ (emission factor × km of that leg)

    Example: 9.9 km by bus + 2.5 km by e-scooter + 0.5 km on foot emits 673 g and scores 69 — not the 879 g and 60 points the same distance would cost by bus alone.

    4How your points add up

    Every journey you complete adds its Green Index to your total: a trip on foot, by bike or by e-scooter is worth 100 points, a bus ride 60. The CO₂ saved is what that same trip would have emitted by car, minus what you actually emitted.

    ⭐Collect more points
    • Walk, cycle or take an e-scooter on short hops — those trips score the full 100.
    • Take the bus instead of the car: 60 points per trip and 102 g of CO₂ saved every kilometre.
    • Sort the routes with the 🌱 Eco chip before you leave — the greenest option comes first.
    • The longer a green trip, the more CO₂ you save: the points depend on the mode, the savings on the distance.
    • Prefer a 🔀 Combined route over the plain bus: the kilometres you cover by bike or e-scooter emit nothing and lift the index of the whole trip.
